How It Works

1

90-Minute Cycles

Each sleep cycle lasts about 90 minutes. You cycle through light sleep, deep sleep, and REM sleep.

2

Wake at Cycle End

Waking up mid-cycle causes grogginess. Waking at the end of a cycle feels natural and refreshing.

3

We Do the Math

We add 15 minutes to fall asleep, then calculate 6 optimal wake-up times across 1-6 sleep cycles.
